Halverd Valley Medical Center (HVMC)
Cumulative Antibiogram, January to December 2025
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT: fictional facility, drugs and devices, created for retrieval testing. Not medical guidance.

The antibiogram reports the percentage of first isolates from each organism that tested susceptible to each antibiotic, one isolate per patient per year. Results are shown only for organisms with at least 30 isolates. Use it to choose empiric therapy before culture results are back; once results are available, narrow therapy to the individual susceptibility report. An agent below 80 percent susceptibility is not recommended for empiric use against that organism.

Trends
ESBL-producing Escherichia coli rose from 11 to 14 percent of urinary isolates. Meronex remains reliable against ESBL organisms and is restricted to protect this. MRSA made up 31 percent of Staphylococcus aureus bloodstream isolates. The antimicrobial stewardship committee reviews these figures every spring and updates the sepsis order set (CP-07) if empiric choices need to change.
