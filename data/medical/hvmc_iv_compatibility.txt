Halverd Valley Medical Center (HVMC)
Y-Site IV Compatibility Chart, 2026
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT: fictional facility, drugs and devices, created for retrieval testing. Not medical guidance.

Y-site compatibility means two drugs can meet in the same IV line for the short time they mix at the connection. It does not mean they can be mixed in the same bag. When a pair is not listed, call central pharmacy at extension 4200 before running them together. Blood products are compatible only with normal saline.

Kaliveen with Heparivex: Compatible at standard concentrations for at least 4 hours.
Kaliveen with Dextriva: Variable: compatible only below the standard concentration; check with pharmacy.
Kaliveen with Fosferra: Compatible at standard concentrations for at least 4 hours.

Section: Magnora Y-site compatibility

Magnora with Prednavex: No data: treat as incompatible until pharmacy confirms.
Magnora with Heparivex: No data: treat as incompatible until pharmacy confirms.
Magnora with Dextriva: Compatible at standard concentrations for at least 4 hours.
Magnora with Fosferra: Compatible at standard concentrations for at least 4 hours.

Section: Prednavex Y-site compatibility

Prednavex with Heparivex: Compatible at the HVMC standard concentrations in the drug library.
Prednavex with Dextriva: Compatible; flush the line with 10 mL saline between doses when running intermittently.
Prednavex with Fosferra: Compatible at the HVMC standard concentrations in the drug library.

Section: Heparivex Y-site compatibility

Heparivex with Dextriva: Compatible at standard concentrations for at least 4 hours.
Heparivex with Fosferra: Incompatible: forms a precipitate. Use a separate lumen or a separate line.

Section: Dextriva Y-site compatibility

Dextriva with Fosferra: Compatible; flush the line with 10 mL saline between doses when running intermittently.
