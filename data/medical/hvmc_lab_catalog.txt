Halverd Valley Medical Center (HVMC)
Laboratory Test Catalog and Reference Ranges, 2026
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT: fictional facility, drugs and devices, created for retrieval testing. Not medical guidance.

Critical value policy
A critical value is a result that may indicate an immediately life-threatening condition. The laboratory phones every critical value to the responsible licensed caregiver within 30 minutes of result verification, and the receiver must read the value back. If the caregiver cannot be reached within 30 minutes, the laboratory escalates to the unit charge nurse and then to the attending physician. Reference ranges below apply to adults.

Specimen rejection
Specimens are rejected if unlabeled, labeled with fewer than two patient identifiers, collected in the wrong tube, clotted when anticoagulated, or underfilled. The lab notifies the unit to redraw.

Section: Chemistry

LC-0410 Sodium, serum
Specimen: lithium heparin (mint green top) tube, minimum 3 mL.
Reference range: 135 to 145 mmol/L.
Critical values: below 120 or above 160 mmol/L.
Turnaround time: STAT 45 minutes; routine 4 hours.
Notes: Correct chronic hyponatremia by no more than 8 mmol/L in 24 hours.

LC-0412 Potassium, serum
Specimen: lithium heparin (mint green top) tube, minimum 3 mL.
Reference range: 3.5 to 5.1 mmol/L.
Critical values: below 2.8 or above 6.2 mmol/L.
Turnaround time: STAT 45 minutes; routine 4 hours.
Notes: Hemolysis falsely raises potassium. A hemolyzed sample is reported with a comment and should be redrawn before treating.

LC-0414 Potassium, whole blood (blood gas analyzer)

Turnaround time: STAT 15 minutes.
Notes: Faster than LC-0412 but not interchangeable with it; use the same method when trending.

Section: Sepsis and infection

LC-0710 Lactate, venous
Specimen: sodium fluoride (gray top) tube, minimum 2 mL.
Reference range: 0.5 to 2.0 mmol/L.
Critical values: 4.0 mmol/L or above.
Turnaround time: STAT 20 minutes.
Notes: Transport on ice and analyze within 15 minutes of collection. Avoid prolonged tourniquet use. If the first lactate is above 2.0 mmol/L, repeat within 2 to 4 hours (CP-07).

LC-0712 Lactate, arterial
Specimen: pre-heparinized arterial syringe, minimum 1 mL.
Reference range: 0.5 to 1.6 mmol/L.
Critical values: 4.0 mmol/L or above.
Turnaround time: STAT 15 minutes.
Notes: Run on the blood gas analyzer; arterial values run slightly lower than venous.

LC-0720 Procalcitonin
Specimen: lithium heparin (mint green top) tube, minimum 3 mL.
Reference range: below 0.10 ng/mL.
Critical values: none defined.
Turnaround time: routine 6 hours.
Notes: A value above 0.5 ng/mL suggests bacterial infection. Used to support stopping antibiotics, not to delay starting them.

LC-0730 Blood culture set
Specimen: one aerobic and one anaerobic bottle, 8 to 10 mL of blood per bottle.
Reference range: no growth.
Critical values: any positive culture.
Turnaround time: preliminary report at 24 hours; final report at 5 days.
Notes: Collect two sets from two separate venipuncture sites before antibiotics are given. Do not draw through an existing IV catheter unless a line infection is suspected.

LC-0732 Urine culture
Specimen: sterile urine cup or catheter sample port, minimum 10 mL.
Reference range: no growth or below 10,000 CFU/mL.
Critical values: none defined.
Turnaround time: preliminary report at 24 hours; final report at 48 hours.
Notes: Never collect from the drainage bag. Refrigerate if not sent within 30 minutes.

LC-0734 Sputum culture
Specimen: sterile sputum cup.
Reference range: normal respiratory flora.
Critical values: none defined.
Turnaround time: final report at 72 hours.
Notes: Rejected if more than 10 squamous cells per field.

LC-0736 MRSA nasal screen (PCR)

LC-0738 Clostridioides difficile toxin PCR

Notes: Only unformed stool is tested; do not test after laxatives within 48 hours.

LC-0740 C-reactive protein (CRP)

Section: Cardiac and endocrine

LC-0815 High-sensitivity troponin (HVMC hs-TnX assay)
Specimen: lithium heparin (mint green top) tube, minimum 3 mL.
Reference range: 99th percentile upper limit 14 ng/L.
Critical values: 52 ng/L or above.
Turnaround time: STAT 45 minutes.
Notes: 0/1-hour algorithm: rule-out if the presentation value is below 5 ng/L and symptoms began more than 3 hours earlier; rule-in if the value is 52 ng/L or above, or rises by 5 ng/L or more at 1 hour. All other patients enter the observation zone and are retested at 3 hours.

Reference range: upper reference limit 30 ng/L.
Critical values: none defined.
Turnaround time: 15 minutes.
Notes: Less sensitive than LC-0815; never used alone to rule out myocardial infarction.
